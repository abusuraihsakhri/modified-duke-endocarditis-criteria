Infective endocarditis classification

Classify already assessed evidence as Definite, Possible, or Rejected IE using the 2023 Duke–ISCVID combinations. One criterion per domain; findings are not inferred from free text or numerical scores.

Clinical-use limitation: This educational aid is not clinically validated software and does not establish or exclude a diagnosis. A negative classification with incomplete investigations does not rule out endocarditis. Confirm the actual definitions in the source criteria and seek specialist clinical assessment.

Pathologic evidence

Definite IE when the specified microorganism or active endocarditis is documented in qualifying tissue/material.

Major clinical criteria

Confirm that each selected category satisfies the published definition. Multiple findings within one category count once.

Minor clinical criteria

Seven domains. Do not count the same microbiologic or imaging finding as both major and minor.

Explicit rejection criteria

Require the published supporting conditions; these may override clinical count combinations.

Assessment

No assessment yet. Review the evidence, confirm completion, and select Classify.

All calculations run in your browser. The page does not upload, store, or transmit criterion selections. Do not enter identifiable patient data.

Decision combinations

  • Definite: pathologic evidence, or 2 major, or 1 major + 3 minor, or 5 minor.
  • Possible: 1 major + 1 minor, or 3 minor.
  • Rejected: an explicit rejection criterion, or failure to satisfy Possible criteria after complete assessment.

The domain definitions (including when blood culture, molecular results, imaging and surgery qualify) must be applied before checking boxes.

Primary reference

Fowler VG Jr et al. Clinical Infectious Diseases. 2023;77(4):518–526.
